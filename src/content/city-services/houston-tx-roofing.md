**The short answer:** Roof replacement in Houston typically costs **$9,500 to $23,000** for a standard 2,000 sq ft home, close to the national average, with storm and hail resistance being the main local factor that shifts material choice and cost. Houston sits close enough to the Gulf Coast to see hurricane-related wind damage and far enough inland to also see significant hail activity, a combination that makes impact-resistant materials a common upgrade here.

## Why Houston Roofs Face a Mix of Storm Risks

Houston's proximity to the Gulf Coast means it faces real hurricane exposure, Hurricane Harvey in 2017 caused widespread wind and water damage across the region even though its primary impact was flooding rather than a direct wind hit. Houston also sits within reach of the broader Texas hail corridor, meaning hail damage claims are common even outside of hurricane season. This dual exposure is different from purely hurricane-driven markets like Miami or purely hail-driven markets like Dallas.

Impact-resistant asphalt shingles have become increasingly common in Houston as a middle-ground choice: more affordable than metal or tile, but rated to better withstand both wind-driven debris and hail impact. Many insurers offer premium discounts for impact-resistant roofing in hail-prone Texas markets. ## Insurance Claims After Storm Damage

If a hurricane or hail event has damaged your roof, document everything with photos before any repair work begins, and get an independent roofing inspection in addition to your insurance adjuster's assessment. Our national [homeowners insurance claims guide](/blog/homeowners-insurance-claims/) walks through the documentation and negotiation process. Be cautious of contractors who show up unsolicited immediately after a storm offering to "handle your insurance claim" directly, this is a documented pattern associated with low-quality or fraudulent roofing work in storm-prone Texas markets.

## Texas Roofing Contractor Verification

Texas does not require a state license specifically for roofing contractors, which makes independent verification of insurance, reputation, and past work especially important in Houston. Check for general liability insurance, ask for references from recent local jobs, and be wary of any contractor pressuring you to sign before your insurance adjuster has assessed the damage.
