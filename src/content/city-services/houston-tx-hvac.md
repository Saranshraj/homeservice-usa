**The short answer:** Central AC installation in Houston typically costs **$3,800 to $8,200 installed**, at the higher end of the national range, because Houston's climate demands larger-capacity systems and more robust humidity control than most of the country. Furnace replacement (needed for winter heat, even in Houston) runs **$2,500 to $6,500** for a standard gas furnace. Emergency AC repair during peak summer months (June through September) commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than the same repair scheduled during Houston's shorter cooling shoulder seasons.

## Why HVAC Costs More to Run in Houston

Houston averages over 100 days a year above 90°F, with summer humidity that regularly pushes the heat index above 105. Air conditioners here run nearly continuously from May through September, sometimes longer. That heavy duty cycle means Houston HVAC systems typically need replacement every 10 to 15 years rather than the 15 to 20 year national average, and repair frequency is higher throughout the system's life.

Humidity control matters as much as temperature here. A system that is undersized for humidity removal, even if it cools the air adequately, can leave a Houston home feeling clammy and create mold risk inside ductwork. When getting quotes in Houston, ask your HVAC contractor specifically about SEER2 rating and humidity control capacity, not just tonnage. | Emergency repair (peak summer) | $350-$1,200 | $250-$900 |
| Annual maintenance plan | $150-$350/year | $120-$300/year |

Houston pricing runs at or slightly above national averages primarily due to system sizing requirements and the sheer volume of run-hours contractors have to warranty against.

## When to Schedule HVAC Work in Houston

Book non-emergency AC replacement in Houston during February, March, October, or November. These shoulder-season months have far lower contractor demand than the June through September peak, when technicians are booked out and emergency call fees are common. If your system is aging and showing early warning signs, our national guide on [deciding whether to repair or replace an AC unit](/blog/repair-or-replace-ac-unit/) can help you plan the timing before a peak-summer failure forces your hand.

## Texas HVAC Contractor Licensing

HVAC contractors operating in Houston must hold a license from the [Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR)](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/ac/ac.htm). You can verify any contractor's license number directly through the TDLR's online search tool before hiring. Given Houston's climate makes AC failure a genuine safety issue in peak summer, verifying licensing and insurance before you need emergency service, not during the emergency, is worth the ten minutes it takes.
