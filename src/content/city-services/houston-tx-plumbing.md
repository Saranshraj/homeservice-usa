**The short answer:** Standard plumbing service calls in Houston run **$175 to $450**, in line with the national average, but Houston has two specific plumbing risk factors that most of the country does not face at the same scale: flood-related sewer backup and occasional hard-freeze pipe bursts. Emergency plumbing during a widespread freeze event, like the February 2021 winter storm, can see wait times of days and pricing well above normal emergency rates due to overwhelming regional demand.

## Why Houston Plumbing Has Unusual Risk Factors

Houston sits in one of the highest flood-risk metro areas in the country. Heavy rain events, common from June through November, can overwhelm municipal sewer systems and cause backup into homes, particularly in low-lying neighborhoods. This is a different problem than a typical clogged drain, and it often requires both plumbing remediation and separate water damage cleanup. If flooding has affected your home, our national [homeowners insurance claims guide](/blog/homeowners-insurance-claims/) explains what flood-related damage is and is not typically covered under a standard policy (flood damage usually requires separate flood insurance).

Houston homes were historically built for heat, not cold, which means pipe insulation and freeze protection are often inadequate by the standards of colder-climate construction. The February 2021 winter storm caused widespread pipe bursts across the Houston metro when temperatures dropped into the teens for several days. While a repeat of that severity is not an annual event, occasional hard freezes do happen, and Houston homeowners who skip basic freeze prep are taking on real risk. Our national guide to [winterizing outdoor faucets](/blog/winterize-outdoor-faucets/) is directly relevant here, even though Houston's typical winter is mild.

## Freeze Preparation Timing

Houston homeowners should winterize exposed pipes and outdoor faucets by early December each year, even though hard freezes are irregular. The cost of prevention (a few hours of work, insulation, and covering exposed pipes) is dramatically lower than the cost of a burst pipe repair plus water damage remediation. See our [winterizing guide](/blog/winterize-outdoor-faucets/) for the full step-by-step process.

## Texas Plumbing Contractor Licensing

Plumbers operating in Houston must be licensed through the [Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners](https://www.tsbpe.texas.gov). You can verify a plumber's license status directly through the board's online lookup tool. This matters particularly after a widespread freeze event, when demand spikes and unlicensed operators sometimes attempt to capitalize on the shortage of available licensed plumbers.
